Teaching is, for me, the other half of doing mathematics: finding the picture or the question that makes an idea click for someone else.
In 2025, the students elected me <strong>Best Teaching Assistant in Mathematics</strong> at KU Leuven.

<h2> Teaching assistant, KU Leuven </h2>
<hr/>

I teach exercise sessions of 25-30 students for the following courses.
<ul>
	<li> Meetkunde II (Geometry 2, differential geometry of surfaces in ℝ³ and elementary algebraic geometry), 3rd bachelor of Mathematics (2023-present) </li>
	<li> Lineaire algebra (Linear algebra), 1st bachelor of Mathematics (2024-present) </li>
	<li> Wiskunde I (Mathematics 1), 1st bachelor of Bio-engineering (2023-2024) </li>
	<li> Wiskunde II (Mathematics 2), 1st bachelor of biochemistry and biotechnology; chemistry; geology (2023-2024) </li>
</ul>

Earlier, I was a student teaching assistant for <i>Mathematical Methods for Biomedical Sciences</i> (2021-2022) and a peer tutor in Quantum Mechanics (2020).
